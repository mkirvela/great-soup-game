The pot, poured out

Tip the whole thing out?

Every ladle you sharpened, every mouth you fed, every creature you ever wrote up — down the drain and forgotten. The fire goes cold. The broth goes grey. The pot, which has simmered since before there was a floor to stand on, forgets your name entirely.

There is no getting it back. That is rather the point of it.
